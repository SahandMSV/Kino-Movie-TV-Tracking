# Why Kino Exists

If you're reading this in the future, I hope you remember why Kino began.

Kino was born from a lifelong love of movies and television. Since childhood, I've been fascinated not only by watching stories but by discovering everything behind them: the people, the history, the craft, and the passion that make cinema what it is.

This project isn't about building just another tracking app. It's about creating a place that helps people discover, remember, and appreciate film and television a little more every day.

## Principles

**Kino is for everyone.** There will never be a premium subscription that hides features behind a paywall. If the project is ever supported financially, it should be through voluntary donations from people who believe in its mission, not because they have to pay to enjoy it.

**Kino is intentionally not open source.** That's not about limiting access; it's about preserving a clear vision while making the application freely available to anyone who wants to use it.

**Kino speaks your language.** Cinema belongs to everyone, so the app should too. It already supports English, German, French and Spanish, and more languages (Persian among them) should follow.

**Your data is yours.** Your watch history, ratings and private notes are for you. Notes are visible only to their author, and integrations like Trakt are optional and use secure, encrypted credentials.

## Staying true

As the project grows, features will come and go. Technologies will change. The mission shouldn't.

Build things that help people enjoy cinema. If a feature serves that purpose, it belongs in Kino.

This is where it all started.
